Elbow Extension Splint

01
Material Strategy
Lightweight aluminum inserts provide rigidity to resist muscle contraction while maintaining a slim profile.Textile Interface: A high-density neoprene laminate sleeve balances compression, comfort, and resistance.
01
Locked State
Sliding the lower aluminum bar upward bridges the clearance gap, engaging the embedded magnetic lock and maintaining a fixed 145° extension.
02
Unlocked State
A downward pull disengages the magnetic lock, restoring the articulation gap and allowing free elbow movement through the fabric.
